# TARANG 2.0 — Raster Visual Asset Pack

This pack contains standalone PNG masters and WebP web-optimised alternatives. Use WebP for most website rendering; retain PNG when you need a lossless master or editing source.

## Folder structure
- `backgrounds/` — full-bleed watercolor and celebration backdrops.
- `category-illustrations/` — six extracted cultural category illustrations. These have an ivory raster background (not transparency); place them on warm ivory/parchment surfaces.
- `decorative-motifs/` — rangoli, arch, lotus, bells, ornamental divider and rhythm wave.
- `patterns/` — subtle woven background and two textile border strips.
- `previews/` — a labelled contact sheet for browsing. Do not use the contact sheet as a website asset.

## Recommended use
- Signature canvas: page background or low-contrast background layer; keep text on a sufficiently opaque content surface.
- Heritage panorama: theme/about feature section; `background-size: cover`, position `center`.
- Dancer portrait: mobile feature/hero side visual, not a full-screen text background.
- Midnight celebration: video/highlight section.
- Woven pattern: use at low opacity or with a parchment overlay; do not use behind dense text.
- Category illustrations: 64–120 px wide on programme category entries.
- Motifs: keep decorative opacity restrained (roughly 0.15–0.35); hide nonessential ornament on narrow screens.

## Asset provenance and limitations
The watercolor background masters are standalone TARANG cultural artwork already present in the project workspace and are included here in a consistent raster package. The category illustrations and decorative motifs were isolated from a newly generated visual sheet. Some icon crops retain an ivory background and are not transparent cut-outs. No official logo, school identity, or event photograph has been modified.

Do not treat decorative background art as event information. Do not add fictional programmes, performers, notices or schedules. Preserve the official TARANG and school logos exactly as supplied elsewhere in the project.
