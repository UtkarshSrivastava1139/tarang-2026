## Brand & Style

This design system translates the cultural gravitas and historic prestige of major biennales, heritage art journals, and national retrospectives (such as the Kochi-Muziris Biennale, Serendipity Arts Festival, and *Marg Magazine*) into a contemporary digital format. It deliberately avoids bright school portal aesthetics, high-saturation commercial gradients, and generic web templates. Instead, the interface establishes an atmosphere of intellectual curation, tactile warmth, and celebratory pride.

The visual direction centers on **Editorial Minimalism with South Asian Heritage Architecture**:
- **Tactile Parchment Ground:** An organic layering of sun-warmed ivory, raw silk, and parchment tones replaces synthetic whites and stark flat backdrops.
- **Asymmetric Curatorial Layouts:** Editorial pacing mirrors exhibition monographs, balancing expansive whitespace with monumental serif typography and documentary photography.
- **Architectural Linework:** Hairline brass and gold dividers, subtle arch frames, and classical proportions structure space without relying on repetitive, enclosed card containers.
- **Institutional Dignity:** Dignified bilingual typography (English and Devanagari) balanced with high-contrast Midnight Navy and ceremonial Vermilion focal accents.

## Colors

The color system is rooted in traditional Indian pigment traditions, tempera manuscripts, and antique textile dyes:

- **Primary (`#17264A` - Midnight Navy):** The structural anchor for typography, mastheads, navigation surfaces, and high-contrast frames. It projects intellectual authority without the coldness of neutral black.
- **Secondary (`#C49A4B` - Antique Gold):** Applied to architectural hairline rules, category badges, active pagination indicators, and ornamental date frames to evoke ceremonial honor.
- **Tertiary (`#AD3025` - Vermilion):** A revered, sacred red reserved for primary calls to action, urgent curatorial notices, and key timestamps.
- **Neutral (`#F7F0E3` - Warm Ivory):** The primary canvas ground, offering the softness of unbleached handmade paper.

### Curatorial Accent Colors
- **Parchment (`#E5D5B8`):** Inset surfaces, metadata backgrounds, and horizontal thematic ribbons.
- **Saffron (`#E98727`), Emerald (`#286B50`), Muted Rose (`#925064`):** Category metadata for disciplines (Music, Performance, Visual Arts, Oral History).
- **Border Hairline (`#E2D3B3`):** High-precision framing rules, separating thematic sections with minimal visual weight.
- **Text Muted (`#56627A`):** Secondary historical notes, publication credits, and inactive tab labels.

## Typography

The type system pairs classical editorial elegance with contemporary functional clarity:

- **Display & Headline Hierarchy:** Handled by `EB Garamond`. Evoking classical letterpress, historical monographs, and stone epigraphy, it is used for exhibition titles, lead quotes, dates, and marquee milestones.
- **Body & Interface Hierarchy:** Handled by `Manrope`. Provides geometric neutrality, open counters, and high legibility across dense schedules, curatorial statements, and mobile screens.
- **Bilingual Typographic Harmony:** When displaying Devanagari text, headings inherit `Noto Serif` styles to match the calligraphic stroke weight of the display headers, while body text and UI labels align with `Noto Sans` metrics.
- **Case & Tracking:** Labels, dates, and category tags utilize uppercase transformations with deliberate positive tracking (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) to mirror archival print conventions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces an asymmetric editorial structure that honors whitespace as an active curatorial element.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - Mobile (below 768px): Fluid single-column container with 16px (`1rem`) outer margins and 16px gutters.
  - Desktop / Tablet (768px+): 12-column asymmetric grid with 24px gutters and generous 48px (`3rem`) canvas margins. Curatorial commentary, primary photography, and timeline markers span irregular column tracks (e.g., 5:7 or 4:8) to break software rigidity.
- **Vertical Spacing:** Generous breathing room separates program blocks (`space-xl`), allowing the eye to process individual cultural offerings without visual crowding.
- **Mobile Clearance:** All views feature an explicit bottom padding of `space-xl` plus 56px to ensure total clearance above fixed navigation elements.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through **Tonal Paper Layering** and **Low-Contrast Hairline Outlines**, rejecting heavy, dark drop shadows.

- **Surface Tiers:** Depth is established by stepping from the base Warm Ivory canvas (`#F7F0E3`) onto subtle Parchment surfaces (`#E5D5B8`) and pristine Ivory cards (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Hairline Framing:** Instead of elevated containers, content groups are demarcated with fine 1px antique gold or sand borders (`#E2D3B3`).
- **Atmospheric Highlights:** Important interactive overlays and floating elements use very faint, warm ambient diffusion: `0 4px 20px rgba(23, 38, 74, 0.06)`. Shadows are warm and tinted with Midnight Navy rather than neutral gray.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft** architectural radius (`level 1`).

- **Structural Forms:** Cards, image viewports, input containers, and button controls use a subtle `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.375rem` (6px) corner radius. This keeps silhouettes crisp, referencing trimmed paper and museum mounts.
- **Curatorial Badges:** Metadata badges and stage categories use pill contours (`rounded-full`) to offer a soft, tactile contrast against rectilinear editorial frames.
- **Architectural Motifs:** Decorative rules and section breaks feature subtle arch or lotus terminal cutouts, retaining authentic cultural character without clutter.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (Ceremonial Vermilion):** Solid `#AD3025` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.25rem` radius, 48px height, 14px Manrope SemiBold. Pressed/hover state shifts to `#8E241B`.
- **Secondary Action (Midnight Frame):** 1px border in `#17264A`, transparent surface, `#17264A` text. Transitions to an 8% navy tint on hover.
- **Curatorial Link:** Clean underline in `#C49A4B`, set in Manrope SemiBold with uppercase tracking, accompanied by an inline right-facing glyph.

### Chips & Badges
- **Discipline Badges (Music, Dance, Art):** 28px height, pill radius, uppercase 11px Manrope Bold (`letterSpacing: 0.08em`).
- **Filter Chips:** 36px height. Inactive state features a `#E5D5B8` parchment ground with `#17264A` text; active state fills with `#17264A` and displays `#FFFFFF` text with a 1px `#C49A4B` highlight border.

### Editorial Schedule Lists & Cards
- **Curatorial Program Item:** Replaces generic drop-shadow cards with an open architectural row. Bordered top and bottom by a 1px hairline rule in `#E2D3B3`. Left column features an EB Garamond timestamp in `#AD3025`; center column contains performance details and artist notes; right column houses action links.
- **Featured Retrospective Card:** Surface-container `#FFFFFF` with 1px border `#E2D3B3`, `0.375rem` radius, padded at `space-md`. Uses an uncropped photographic hero ratio (16:10), followed by an EB Garamond headline and a hairline gold divider.

### Input Fields
- Height 48px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#E2D3B3`, text in `#17264A`. On focus, transitions cleanly to a 1px `#C49A4B` border with a subtle warm gold highlight glow, avoiding standard system blue.

### Mobile Navigation Dock
- 56px height, pinned to viewport base. Solid `#17264A` background with a continuous 1px `#C49A4B` hairline top rule.
- Five touch targets (minimum 44 × 44px). Inactive icons render in 60% opacity Warm Ivory; the active item displays luminous Antique Gold (`#C49A4B`) with a centered 3px gold indicator dot beneath.
