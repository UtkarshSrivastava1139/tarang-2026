Nurturing Character through India’s Living Arts

Founded under the auspices of Smt. Radhika Devi Intermediate College & Children Academy, TARANG was instituted in 2011 as a moral and cultural counterpoint to rote academics. Mathauli Bazar serves as our sanctuary where timeless Indian aesthetics — Ras, Dhwani, and Nritya — mould disciplined, articulate leaders.
