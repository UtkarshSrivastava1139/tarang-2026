ACCESSION #2024-NATYA-01 Plate I: 'Ritu Vasant'

Classical Kathak & Bharatanatyam Invocation

STAGE A • DURATION 18:40

Curated under the tutelage of senior performing arts faculty, this opening invocation synthesized rhythmic tatkar footwork with expressive abhinaya. The lighting architecture deliberately contrasted deep indigo stage flanks with warm vermilion floodlights, capturing the ceremonial transcendence of eastern Uttar Pradesh youth traditions.
