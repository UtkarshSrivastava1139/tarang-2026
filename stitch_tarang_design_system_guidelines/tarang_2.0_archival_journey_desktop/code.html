Our Moral & Cultural Compass

Nurturing Soil, Civilizational Horizons

Founded under the visionary patronage of Smt. Radhika Devi Intermediate College and Smt. Radhika Devi Children Academy, TARANG represents more than a stage festival; it is a sacred pedagogy where moral rectitude meets aesthetic grandeur.

Situated amidst the historically serene plains of Mathauli Bazar in Kushinagar—an ancient seat of contemplative thought and enlightenment—our academy holds the firm conviction that genuine scholarship requires an awakened aesthetic conscience.

"We prepare our young scholars not merely to pass examinations of state curricula, but to hold the custody of India’s living cultural ethos—her ragas, her moral verse, and her unyielding fellowship."

Curatorial Sequence

Roadmap to TARANG 2.0 Assembly

From the first institutional resolution to the grand national stage in Kushinagar.

Milestone 01: Official Conclave Declaration Completed • August 2026

Governing Council and Institutional Faculty of Smt. Radhika Devi Intermediate College ratified the 2026 Curatorial Charter, fixing the theme and delegating administrative committees.

Milestone 02: Intra-Institutional Talent Auditions Completed • September 2026

Over 1,200 students across junior and senior wings auditioned across classical vocal, instrumental percussion, theatrical elocution, folk arts, and visual gallery exhibitions.

Milestone 03: Classical Raga & Choreography Camps Active Phase • October 2026

Master workshops supervised by esteemed visiting gurus from Varanasi and Gorakhpur. Daily rehearsals for the 18-minute symphonic overture combining 40 acoustic instruments.

mic Camp Venue: Main Auditorium & Open Air Amphitheatre, Mathauli Campus
Milestone 04: Stage Technical Assembly & Dress Rehearsals Scheduled • 14–16 November 2026

Acoustic sound balancing, traditional wooden stage rigging, illumination checks, and full sequential dry runs in festival ceremonial costumes.

Milestone 05: The Grand Assembly — TARANG 2.0 18 November 2026 • 3:00 PM IST

The monumental live convocation. Dignitaries, educationalists, alumni, parents, and civil guests assemble for the live thematic pageant and multi-discipline cultural performances.

Milestone 06: Retrospective Monograph & Gazette Release Archive • December 2026

Release of high-definition digital recordings, student scholarly essays, photo journals, and institutional distribution of commemorative merit honors.
