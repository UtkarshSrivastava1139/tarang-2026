Official Protocol Compendium & FAQs

CHAPTER IV

01. How are physical invitation passes verified at the entrance?

Each printed card contains an authorized seal and house allotment counter-foil. Guests must display the original physical card at Gate 2. Digital snapshots or SMS messages are strictly invalid for entry.

02. What is the solemn dress code for attendees and guardians?

In keeping with the institutional dignity and cultural sanctity of TARANG 2.0, traditional Indian attire (Kurta-Pyjama, Dhoti-Kurta, Sarees, Salwar-Kameez) or formal academic attire is enthusiastically recommended for all patrons.

03. Are personal cameras and independent live-streaming permitted?

No independent DSLRs, tripods, or drones are allowed without an authorized press pass issued by the Media Secretariat. The conclave will be recorded in multi-cam 4K for the permanent YouTube institutional archive.

04. How is student security and dispersal supervised post-performance?

Participating students remain within the secure Greenroom Enclosure until the finale national anthem. Parents may only receive their children through the designated House Master Handover Desk at Gate 3.

05. Can alumni and prospective patrons register for future sessions?

Yes. An Alumni Liaison Desk will be active in the central library vestibule during the conclave. Members can record their archival contributions and receive future publications.

06. What medical and first-aid provisions are stationed on campus?

A dedicated medical dispensary staffed by a medical officer and equipped with an emergency ambulance will be stationed adjacent to Pavilion East throughout the program duration.
