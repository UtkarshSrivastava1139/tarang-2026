## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authentic bridge between classical Indian cultural heritage and disciplined modern digital editorial craft. It departs strictly from garish festival kitsch, loud multi-color gradients, or institutional school portal aesthetics. Instead, the interface treats cultural celebration as a curated fine-arts program—graceful, prestigious, warm, and highly functional on mobile viewports.

The visual style combines **Editorial Minimalism** with **Traditional Indian Heritage Artistry**:
- Tactile, sun-drenched paper textures implied through warm, organic color layering rather than heavy drop shadows.
- Disciplined typographic hierarchy with classical high-contrast serif headlines paired with precise geometric sans-serif UI controls.
- Delicate Antique Gold borders, traditional filigree separators, and subtle architectural arch motifs framing structured event programming.
- High touch-accessibility standards engineered for parents, alumni, dignitaries, and students across dynamic bilingual contexts (English and Hindi).

## Colors

The palette draws directly from heritage manuscript illumination, combining warm tactile grounds with deep imperial navy and regal gold.

- **Primary (`#17264A` - Midnight Navy):** Serves as the bedrock of typography, high-contrast dark surfaces, and navigation elements. It provides authoritative grounding without the coldness of pure black.
- **Secondary (`#C49A4B` - Antique Gold):** Applied to ornamental filigree lines, borders, active indicators, and section badges to convey prestige and ceremonial elegance.
- **Tertiary (`#AD3025` - Vermilion):** The focal interactive color reserved for primary calls to action, urgent event alerts, and primary interactive states.
- **Neutral (`#F7F0E3` - Warm Ivory):** The primary canvas background, creating an inviting, paper-like warmth.
- **Named Accents:**
  - `parchment` (`#E5D5B8`): Container fills, timeline tracks, and subtle inset backgrounds.
  - `saffron` (`#E98727`), `emerald` (`#286B50`), and `muted-rose` (`#925064`): Curated category tags (e.g., Music, Dance, Drama, Art).
  - `text-muted` (`#56627A`): Secondary meta copy, captions, and inactive tab states.

## Typography

The typography system uses a disciplined dual-stack pairing:
- **English Display & Headlines:** Classical serif rendered via `EB Garamond`, offering high legibility, graceful flourishes, and literary authority.
- **English UI, Controls & Body:** Modern neutral clarity via `Manrope`, maintaining crisp legibility at small sizes and high scanning efficiency across dense schedules.
- **Devanagari Bilingual Pairings:** Where Hindi is rendered, headings automatically inherit `Noto Serif Devanagari` to preserve the calligraphic stroke weight of the display headers, while Hindi interface controls and body text map to `Noto Sans Devanagari` at equivalent line-height baselines.

Official brandmarks, including the event title logo and the Hindi theme artwork (`एक भारत • अनेक रंग`), are preserved strictly as pristine graphic assets and never approximated with editable typography.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy is mobile-first, single-column responsive, expanding into an editorial column grid on wider screens.

- **Mobile Viewport (360px–430px):** Content conforms to a fluid container with 16px (`1rem`) outer margins and 16px gutters. Every primary interactive element guarantees a minimum touch footprint of 44 × 44px. 
- **Desktop/Tablet Breakpoints (768px+ and 1024px+):** The single-column flow reorganizes into an asymmetrical editorial layout with 24px gutters and generous 48px margins, accommodating photo essays, multi-stage performance schedules, and archival records.
- **Rhythm & Bottom Clearance:** Because of the persistent 56px fixed bottom navigation bar on mobile, all scroll views include an automatic padding-bottom of `space-xl` + 56px to prevent content occlusion.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses subtle, soft rounding (level `1`). Curvature remains restrained to preserve the architectural and editorial seriousness of a cultural institution.

- Base controls, cards, input frames, and category chips use `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.5rem` (8px) radii.
- Badges and category indicator pills use fully rounded capsule contours to contrast with sharp rectangular text frames.
- Ornamental dividers may feature subtle central lotus cuts or pointed temple-arch finials drawn in delicate 1px gold strokes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Background in `Vermilion` (`#AD3025`), text in `#FFFFFF`, with `0.25rem` corner radius, 14px Manrope SemiBold, 48px minimum height, and 24px horizontal padding. Hover/active shifts to a deeper crimson shade (`#8E241B`).
- **Secondary Button:** Outlined with a 1px border in `Midnight Navy` (`#17264A`), text in `Midnight Navy`, transparent background, shifting to a 10% navy tint on press.
- **Ceremonial CTA:** Background in `Midnight Navy` (`#17264A`) with an inset 1px `Antique Gold` border and gold text (`#C49A4B`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Cultural Category Chips:** 32px height, 12px Manrope SemiBold, fully rounded pill radius. Inactive chips use a `Parchment` background with `Midnight Navy` text; active chips adopt the specific category accent (`Saffron`, `Emerald`, or `Muted Rose`) with white text.

### Cards
- **Editorial Event Card:** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), 1px hairline border in `#E2D3B3`, `0.375rem` radius, padded at `space-md`. Contains category badge top-right, Garamond display heading, performance timestamp in `text-muted`, and a fine 1px gold divider line separating the presenter's credentials.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Height 48px. Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#E2D3B3`, text in `Midnight Navy`. Focus state transitions the border to `Antique Gold` (`#C49A4B`) with a 1px gold outer ring, never generic blue.

### Bottom Navigation Bar (Mobile)
- Fixed at viewport bottom, 56px height, 44px+ hit areas. Solid `Midnight Navy` (`#17264A`) surface with a 1px top border in `Antique Gold` (`#C49A4B`).
- 5 destinations (*Home*, *Programme*, *Journey*, *Gallery*, *More*). Inactive icons render in 60% opacity Warm Ivory; active item renders in luminous Antique Gold with a subtle gold dot indicator below.
