04 / Patron Inquiries Q&A DIRECTORY
Is there an entry fee or digital ticket required? expand_more
No. TARANG 2.0 is an institutional cultural celebration. Admission is completely free, governed by the ceremonial physical Patron Invitation Card distributed through registered students.
Can non-student community members attend? expand_more
Attendance is prioritized for parents, guardians, verified alumni of both institutions, and official academic dignitaries of Kushinagar district. Extra seats in the balcony quadrangle open at 02:30 PM.
What are the camera and recording protocols? expand_more
Flash photography is strictly prohibited during classical classical dance and solo theatrical recitals to avoid blinding performers. The entire festival will be archived and livestreamed on the official YouTube channel.
How may accredited press or journalists register? expand_more
Accredited journalists may present press credentials at the Secretariat Desk at Gate No. 1 between 12:00 PM and 01:45 PM on November 18 for official press badges and reserved media tier seating.
